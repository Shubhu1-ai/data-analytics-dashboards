# Robotics Adoption & Industry Impact (2015–2023)

A Power BI dashboard analyzing how robotics adoption has affected productivity, cost, and the workforce across manufacturing, healthcare, and logistics between 2015 and 2023.

**Data:** Sample dataset built for this analysis (not real company data).

## What it looks at
- Robots adopted per industry, per year
- Productivity gain (%)
- Cost savings over time
- Jobs displaced by automation
- Training hours invested per industry

## Key insights
- **8,219 robots** were adopted across the tracked industries, driving an average productivity gain of **15%**.
- **Healthcare leads adoption** at 38.6%, followed by logistics (33.5%) and manufacturing (28%) — healthcare's biggest gains came later, with a sharp jump in cost savings in 2023.
- **Manufacturing saw the earliest returns**, with strong cost savings appearing early in the timeline versus healthcare's later surge.
- **Training investment correlates with smoother adoption** — training hours peaked in 2016 and 2020, aligning with the industries' major adoption phases.
- **Logistics saw the highest job displacement** (528 roles), followed closely by healthcare and manufacturing — a reminder that productivity gains and workforce impact move together, not independently.

## Why this project
Robotics adoption isn't just an engineering question — it's a business one. This dashboard was built to quantify the trade-offs (productivity and cost savings vs. workforce displacement) that come with automating an industry, and to practice translating that into something decision-makers can actually act on.
